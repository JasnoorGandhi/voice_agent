CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
================================

RETURN POLICY
-------------
- We offer a 30-day return policy on all products.
- Items must be in original condition with tags and packaging intact.
- To start a return: log into your account, go to Order History, click Return Items.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.
- Non-returnable items: personalized products, digital downloads, gift cards, opened cosmetics, final sale items.

SHIPPING
--------
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50.
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $12.99.
- Next-day shipping: 1 business day, $24.99.
- Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same day.
- International shipping: 7-14 business days via DHL, available to 50+ countries.
- You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

ORDER MANAGEMENT
----------------
- Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement.
- Once shipped, orders cannot be modified — you may return the item instead.
- To track your order: log into your account and go to Order History, or use the tracking number emailed to you.

PAYMENTS
--------
- We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
- Buy Now Pay Later available via Klarna and Afterpay.
- All payments are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

WARRANTY
--------
- Electronics: 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects and malfunctions.
- Apparel and accessories: 90-day warranty against material defects.
- Warranty claims require proof of purchase within the warranty period.
